## Verdict

**Partially implemented. The prompt's final acceptance condition is not met.**

The working receipt path is image/text extraction → supplier/sales-agent suggestions → editable review → explicit confirmation → purchase receipt and optional inventory receipt **inside the scanning business**. It preserves document/product/movement links and protects repeated confirmation of the same job.

There is also a separate, reusable multi-party source/offer/order/fulfillment flow. It supports authenticated representation decisions, territory metadata, accepted distribution relationships and source-linked invoice fulfillment. It is not wired to make a receipt scan update the retailer, wholesaler, salesperson and financial projections as one recoverable transaction.

Do not count a supplier or sales-agent contact stored in the retailer's database as an update to that participant's own authenticated account. Do not count a documentary network graph or `COMPLETED` OCR job as evidence of committed synchronization across accounts.

## P0 — correctness and authorization blockers

These are implementation gaps inferred from the inspected paths, not claims that a production exploit was reproduced.

- [x] **Enforce document classification before stock or financial posting.** Persist purchase/sale/return/refund/credit-note/quotation/pro-forma/delivery/payment-receipt types and validate their allowed effects. `readChatImage` currently classifies only delivery receipt, purchase receipt or other; confirmation accepts a `receiveInventory` flag without a persisted comprehensive transaction/fulfillment policy. Quotations must not move stock, and invoices must not imply payment. **Done 2026-11-24:** `receipt-validation.ts` names eleven document types and what each may post (`receiptDocumentEffects`): only purchase receipts, delivery notes and invoices post; quotations and pro-formas never do; returns, refunds and credit notes are refused until reversals exist; an invoice records what is owed, never a payment. The type is Luna's reading cross-checked against the printed heading (disagreement: a person chooses), the heading alone for text, or an assumed purchase receipt for an unheaded slip, recorded in `derivedFields`. Confirmation recomputes it from the draft (`assertReceiptPostable`, both the inventory and plain paths). Evidence: `tests/receipt-posting-rules.test.ts`, `tests/receipt-validation.test.ts`; eval `pnpm eval:receipt-reading` (Luna on rendered receipts, 5/5 three runs).
- [x] **Add independent financial validation.** Validate every quantity × unit-price calculation, line discounts, tax basis, rounding, subtotal, grand total, paid amount and balance. `suppliers/shared.ts:717` currently warns only when the sum of line totals differs from the receipt total by more than 1. There is no full line/tax/discount validator or required discrepancy-resolution gate. **Done 2026-11-24:** `validateReceiptArithmetic` recomputes every line (quantity × unit price), lines against subtotal, subtotal less discount with tax inclusive or added against total, and total less paid against balance (change included), to half a cent. Confirmation refuses any mismatch until it is corrected or ticked as checked against the paper (`acknowledgedDiscrepancies`, saved on the receipt); the checks are server-computed and shown in review (`ReceiptChecksPanel`). Also fixed: printed amounts with thousands commas ("3,800") were read as 3.80. Evidence: `tests/receipt-validation.test.ts`, `tests/receipt-posting-rules.test.ts`, `tests/receipt-checks-ui.test.tsx`.
- [x] **Distinguish printed, unknown and derived values.** `suppliers/shared.ts:239` computes a missing line total from quantity × unit price; `suppliers/store.ts` substitutes the current date for a missing receipt date. Preserve uncertainty/provenance or require a correction instead of representing defaults as printed financial evidence. **Done 2026-11-24:** a computed line total is recorded in `derivedFields` (kept while it still equals its calculation, dropped once a person edits it), never checked against itself, and labelled "calculated, not printed" in review; Luna is told to copy printed amounts exactly and list any it computed. A missing receipt date or total is still filled in at confirmation (the confirmation time, the line sum), and the saved receipt lists them as derived (`receipt.purchaseDate`, `receipt.total`). Evidence: `tests/receipt-posting-rules.test.ts`.
- [~] **Reject unit mismatches until an explicit conversion is verified.** `store.ts:9565` adds `item.quantity` directly to the matched product's quantity, without checking the receipt unit against the stock unit. Five cartons must not be added as five packets. Add validated pack definitions, conversion provenance and the 5 × 12 = 60 acceptance case; unknown conversions must stay pending. **Partly done 2026-11-20:** a receipt line whose unit differs from the matched product's stock unit (or from another line for the same new product) now refuses confirmation with `delivery_unit_mismatch` and posts nothing, so the draft stays pending for correction (`receipt-units.ts`, `tests/delivery-receipt-units.test.ts`, including the 5 cartons → 60 packets correction). Products saved without a unit (placeholder "unit") are not contradicted. Still open: verified pack definitions with conversion provenance, so a receipt in cartons can be converted automatically.
- [ ] **Deduplicate the same commercial receipt across different photographs/text.** Current draft deduplication uses a same-business image hash, or filename/content-type/text hash; confirmation reuses the same job's purchase receipt. This does not establish merchant + receipt number + amount/date identity across recaptures, crops or altered OCR. Add semantic fingerprints, database uniqueness/idempotency constraints, conflict review and concurrent-scan tests.
- [ ] **Link verified participant accounts explicitly.** A retailer's local `supplierId`/`salesAgentId` is not an authenticated wholesaler/salesperson account. Add an explicit, consented mapping from documentary contacts to verified businesses/accounts; ties and conflicting evidence must remain pending. Never use OCR names/phone numbers as authority.
- [ ] **Extend the existing canonical records with a receipt transaction identity and participant projections.** Link existing purchase receipts, orders/invoices, product/movement references and source bindings; retain buyer/seller/salesperson identities, source fingerprint, currency, amounts, document type, verification, authorization, fulfillment and per-participant sync states. Avoid another independent inventory or invoice ledger.
- [ ] **Implement trusted trading policies.** Extend existing authenticated relationships/representation grants with allowed categories, value ceilings, inventory/payment permissions, salesperson/delegation limits, expiry and revocation. Evaluate current permissions on execution and retry; previously accepted contact or distribution links are not blanket posting authority.
- [ ] **Build receipt-specific durable synchronization.** Persist transaction and participant work/outbox records atomically with authorized local effects. Add idempotent consumers, retries, failed/dead-letter handling and reconciliation. General daemon queues, messaging outboxes, realtime sync and fulfillment outboxes do not themselves synchronize receipt accounts.
- [ ] **Tie success to committed effects.** Track each participant's committed projection, not merely OCR status or an in-memory mutation. Preserve uncertain commit outcomes and reconcile without replaying stock movements. Make the final success response conditional on required committed projections; show partial/pending states otherwise.
- [ ] **Add receipt reversals and disputes.** Completed OCR jobs cannot be edited, which is a useful protection, but there is no receipt-specific compensating stock/financial workflow. Corrections after posting must reference the original transaction and preserve history.

## P1 — account projections and intelligence

- [ ] **Retailer projection:** integrate receipt purchase history/stock with verified wholesaler and salesperson links, payable entries, partial payment and fulfillment rules. Current purchase receipt saving does not create the requested payable ledger.
- [ ] **Wholesaler projection:** create the authorized sale/invoice, confirmed stock decrement, receivable and salesperson attribution from the same receipt transaction. Reuse source fulfillment and invoice/payment services; do not decrement stock twice if a related order already posted it.
- [ ] **Salesperson projection:** update the salesperson's own attributed sales/customer assignments and configured commission eligibility. `SalesAgentSummary.receiptsHandled` is a local supplier-contact statistic, not the salesperson's account performance or commission ledger. Representation and territory metadata already exist and should be extended.
- [ ] **Agent projection:** publish authorized transaction events after commit, refresh permitted context and schedule bounded follow-up/reconciliation tasks through the existing daemon/harness. Do not grant a model a direct ledger mutation path.

- [ ] **Use real field uncertainty.** Luna extraction currently requires review, and generic field evidence uses fixed confidence constants for a small set of fields (`suppliers/shared.ts:665`). Add evidence/confidence for each relevant field and product, contradictions and missing-line indicators. Do not silently omit unreadable lines from a supposedly complete transaction.
- [ ] **Integrate the receipt matching hierarchy.** Reuse catalogue aliases/search and source identity helpers for barcode, supplier-specific codes, namespaced SKU, historical purchases, normalized descriptions and ranked fuzzy candidates. Require review where identities conflict; preserve existing exact-SKU/name matching as the conservative base.
- [ ] **Propose missing products without premature creation.** Current receipt confirmation can create an unmatched product with its buying price. Add reviewed proposals and authorized supplier-catalogue suggestions, explicit units and duplicate/low-confidence resolution before posting.
- [ ] **Handle unregistered partners and later claims.** Preserve local supplier records and documentary evidence; add verified account-claiming and explicit historical-link consent. Do not manufacture authenticated accounts or retroactively expose another store's private receipts or move stock.
- [ ] **Preserve runtime selection consistently.** Reuse OpenRouter Luna and existing user-selected conversation runtimes. The general image endpoint defaults directly to Luna; linked-WhatsApp image reading separately resolves the selected runtime. Verify/document the intended receipt behavior after an explicit model/engine change.
