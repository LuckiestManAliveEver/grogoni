# Grogoni Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (Draft)
**Part III – International Data Transfers, Data Retention, Information Security, Data Subject Rights, Automated Decision-Making, and Children's Privacy**

---

# 19. International Transfers of Personal Data

Grogoni may process Personal Data in countries other than the country in which it was originally collected.

Where Personal Data is transferred internationally, we will implement appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, safeguards may include:

- adequacy decisions adopted by competent authorities;
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs);
- approved codes of conduct;
- approved certification mechanisms;
- other lawful transfer mechanisms recognized under applicable data protection laws.

Where legally required, users may request additional information regarding applicable transfer safeguards.

---

# 20. Data Storage

Personal Data may be stored using cloud infrastructure, data centers, or other secure processing environments operated by Grogoni or authorized service providers.

Storage locations may change over time to improve:

- availability;
- reliability;
- disaster recovery;
- latency;
- legal compliance.

Where reasonably practicable, data residency options may be made available for business or enterprise customers.

---

# 21. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- provide the Services;
- comply with legal obligations;
- resolve disputes;
- enforce agreements;
- detect fraud;
- maintain security;
- satisfy accounting and tax requirements.

Retention periods vary depending upon:

- the type of information;
- the Services used;
- applicable legal obligations;
- operational requirements.

---

## 21.1 Account Information

Account information is generally retained for the duration of the user's relationship with Grogoni.

Following account closure, information may be retained where necessary for:

- legal compliance;
- fraud prevention;
- dispute resolution;
- security investigations;
- backup restoration.

---

## 21.2 Messaging Data

Conversation history may be retained for the period necessary to provide messaging functionality, synchronize devices, restore conversations where applicable, investigate abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Users may have tools to delete or manage portions of their conversation history, subject to legal or operational limitations.

---

## 21.3 AI Interaction Data

AI interaction data may be retained only for the period necessary to:

- provide requested AI functionality;
- maintain conversation context where enabled;
- improve platform reliability where legally permitted;
- detect abuse;
- investigate security incidents.

Where AI interaction data is used to improve services, it will be processed in accordance with applicable law and any choices made available to users.

---

## 21.4 OCR and Uploaded Documents

Uploaded receipts, invoices, and business documents are retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality or until removed by the user, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Users are responsible for managing uploaded business records in accordance with their own legal obligations.

---

## 21.5 Marketplace Information

Marketplace listings, transaction records, ratings, and licensing information may be retained after account closure where necessary to:

- maintain transaction records;
- satisfy legal obligations;
- resolve disputes;
- protect users;
- maintain the integrity of the Marketplace.

---

## 21.6 Account Deletion and Recovery

When a user requests account deletion:

- account access may be disabled immediately;
- recoverable account data may be retained for **up to thirty (30) days** to permit recovery in the event of accidental deletion;
- following the recovery period, Personal Data scheduled for deletion will be permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized unless continued retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

Certain records may be retained beyond the recovery period where legally required, including accounting records, fraud prevention records, and information necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

---

# 22. Information Security

Protecting Personal Data is a core responsibility of Grogoni.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Personal Data.

Examples may include:

- encryption in transit;
- encryption at rest where appropriate;
- role-based access controls;
- multi-factor authentication;
- security monitoring;
- audit logging;
- vulnerability management;
- regular software updates;
- incident response procedures;
- backup and disaster recovery systems.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection, and users also play an important role in safeguarding their accounts and devices.

---

# 23. Data Subject Rights

Where applicable law provides such rights, users may have the right to:

- obtain confirmation that Personal Data is being processed;
- access Personal Data;
- correct inaccurate Personal Data;
- complete incomplete Personal Data;
- request deletion of Personal Data;
- restrict processing;
- object to certain processing;
- receive Personal Data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

The availability of these rights depends on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing activity.

---

## 23.1 Right of Access

Users may request confirmation regarding whether Personal Data is processed and obtain access to the Personal Data held by Grogoni, subject to applicable legal limitations.

---

## 23.2 Right to Rectification

Users may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data.

Where possible, users should update account information directly through available account settings.

---

## 23.3 Right to Erasure

Users may request deletion of Personal Data in circumstances provided by applicable law.

Deletion requests remain subject to:

- legal retention obligations;
- fraud prevention;
- security requirements;
- dispute resolution;
- defense of legal claims.

---

## 23.4 Right to Restrict Processing

Users may request restriction of processing in circumstances recognized by applicable law.

---

## 23.5 Right to Data Portability

Where applicable, users may request a copy of certain Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

---

## 23.6 Right to Object

Users may object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes where applicable.

Grogoni will evaluate objections in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 23.7 Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing is based on consent, users may withdraw consent at any time.

Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before consent was withdrawn.

---

# 24. Automated Decision-Making

Certain platform functionality may involve automated processing, including:

- fraud detection;
- spam prevention;
- AI-generated responses;
- recommendation systems;
- workflow automation.

Where applicable law grants users rights relating to automated decision-making, Grogoni will provide appropriate safeguards, including opportunities for human review where legally required.

Grogoni does not intentionally use solely automated decision-making to produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals unless authorized by applicable law and accompanied by appropriate safeguards.

---

# 25. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed toward children who are below the minimum age required to enter into a binding agreement under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children where such collection is prohibited by law.

If we become aware that Personal Data has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information without undue delay.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided Personal Data unlawfully may contact us using the information provided in this Privacy Policy.

---

# 26. Data Breach Response

Grogoni maintains procedures for identifying, investigating, responding to, and mitigating personal data breaches.

Where required by applicable law, we will:

- notify competent supervisory authorities within the legally required timeframes;
- notify affected individuals where legally required;
- document the incident;
- implement appropriate remediation measures.

Not every security incident constitutes a reportable personal data breach under applicable law.

---

# 27. Security Responsibilities of Users

Users also contribute to protecting Personal Data by:

- maintaining strong passwords or passkeys;
- enabling multi-factor authentication where available;
- protecting devices from unauthorized access;
- promptly reporting suspected account compromise;
- keeping software up to date.

Failure to follow reasonable security practices may increase the risk of unauthorized access to user accounts.
