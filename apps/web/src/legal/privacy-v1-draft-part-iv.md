# 28. Exercising Your Privacy Rights

Users may exercise applicable privacy rights by contacting Grogoni using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy or through available account management tools.

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the request, we may request reasonable information necessary to verify the identity of the requester before responding.

Where permitted by law, requests submitted by authorized representatives may require evidence of authorization.

We aim to respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

---

# 29. Complaints

If you believe that your Personal Data has been processed in a manner inconsistent with applicable law or this Privacy Policy, we encourage you to contact Grogoni first so that we may investigate and attempt to resolve your concerns.

Submitting a complaint to Grogoni does not affect any statutory right to contact a supervisory authority or seek another legal remedy.

---

# 30. Supervisory Authorities

Where the GDPR or similar legislation applies, individuals have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority in the country of their habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

Grogoni will cooperate with competent supervisory authorities as required by applicable law.

# 31. Cookies and Preference Management

Users may manage cookie preferences through:

- cookie preference tools provided by Grogoni, where available;
- browser settings;
- operating system privacy controls;
- device-specific privacy settings.

Disabling certain cookies or similar technologies may reduce the availability or functionality of certain Services.

Where consent is required for non-essential cookies, users may withdraw or modify their cookie preferences at any time.

# 33. Business Customers

Where merchants use Grogoni to interact with customers, the merchant may act as an independent controller of Personal Data collected through its storefront or business operations.

In such circumstances:

- the merchant is responsible for complying with applicable privacy laws in relation to its own processing activities;
- Grogoni may act as a processor, controller, or independent controller depending on the particular processing activity and contractual relationship;
- additional contractual terms, including a Data Processing Addendum where appropriate, may apply.

# 34. Cross-Border Business Operations

Because Grogoni may support international commerce, communications, and AI services, Personal Data may be processed across multiple jurisdictions.

# 35. Artificial Intelligence Transparency

Grogoni seeks to provide transparency regarding the use of artificial intelligence within the Services.

- AI-generated responses may be identified where appropriate;
- users remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before relying on it for significant decisions;
- AI systems may produce inaccurate or incomplete information;
- human review may occur in limited circumstances described in this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Where applicable law requires additional AI transparency measures, Grogoni will implement them.

# Annex C – Data Retention Summary

Retention periods depend on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

Examples include:

- Account data: retained during the account lifecycle and thereafter only as necessary.
- Messaging data: retained as required to provide messaging functionality and meet legal obligations.
- Uploaded documents: retained until deleted by the user or no longer required for the requested functionality.
- Marketplace records: retained where necessary for transaction history, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
- Deleted accounts: recoverable for up to **30 days**, after which Personal Data is permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized unless continued retention is required or permitted by law.
