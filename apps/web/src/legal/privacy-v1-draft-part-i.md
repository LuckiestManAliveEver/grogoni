# PART I – OVERVIEW, SCOPE, DEFINITIONS, DATA CONTROLLER, AND PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Grogoni** ("**Grogoni**," "**we**," "**our**," or "**us**").

Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Grogoni collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and otherwise processes Personal Data when you use our websites, applications, APIs, AI services, messaging platform, storefront technology, marketplace, and related services (collectively, the "**Services**").

This Privacy Policy is intended to satisfy applicable data protection laws, including, where applicable:

- the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR");
- the UK GDPR;
- the ePrivacy rules applicable in relevant jurisdictions;
- national data protection legislation implementing or supplementing these laws.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Grogoni Terms of Service and any additional notices provided for specific products or features.

# 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies whenever you:

- create a Grogoni account;
- browse our website;
- use our mobile applications;
- communicate with AI Business Agents;
- communicate with merchants through the platform;
- create a storefront;
- operate a business account;

# 5. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

The categories of Personal Data we collect depend on how you use the Services.

---

## 5.1 Identity Information

We may collect:

- name;
- display name;
- username;
- Business Agent ID;
- business name;
- profile photograph;
- date of birth where required;
- verification information.

---

## 5.2 Contact Information

We may collect:

- email address;
- telephone number;
- mailing address;
- billing address;
- business contact information.

---

## 5.3 Authentication Information

We may process information relating to authentication, including:

- password hashes;
- one-time password (OTP) verification records;
- passkey identifiers;
- multi-factor authentication settings;
- authentication tokens;
- login timestamps.

We do not intentionally store plaintext passwords.

---

## 5.4 Business Information

For business accounts we may collect:

- business descriptions;
- product catalogues;
- pricing;
- inventory information;
- order information;
- payment preferences;
- tax identifiers where required;
- storefront branding;
- customer service information.

---

## 5.5 Communications

We may process communications exchanged through the Services, including:

- customer conversations;
- merchant communications;
- AI conversations;
- uploaded files;
- images;
- voice messages;
- customer support requests.

Communications may be processed to provide requested functionality, improve service quality, maintain security, investigate abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

---

## 5.6 AI Interaction Data

When users interact with AI Business Agents or other AI features, we may process:

- prompts;
- instructions;
- uploaded documents;
- generated responses;
- user feedback;
- conversation context;
- interaction metadata.

AI interactions may be processed to provide the requested service, improve system performance where permitted, detect abuse, and maintain platform security.

---

## 5.7 Receipt and OCR Data

Where users upload receipts, invoices, or business documents, we may process information extracted through optical character recognition (OCR), including:

- merchant names;
- transaction dates;
- product descriptions;
- quantities;
- prices;
- taxes;
- totals;
- invoice references;
- payment details;
- contact information contained in the uploaded document.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload such documents.

---

## 5.8 Contact Synchronization Data

If users choose to synchronize contacts, we may process:

- names;
- telephone numbers;
- email addresses;
- contact labels;
- relationship metadata necessary to provide synchronization features.

Contact synchronization is optional and subject to user consent or another appropriate legal basis where required by applicable law.

---

## 5.9 Social Media Integration Data

If users connect supported third-party platforms, we may receive information authorized by the user through the relevant provider, such as:

- account identifiers;
- profile names;
- profile photographs;
- messaging permissions;
- business page information;
- authentication tokens;
- other data explicitly authorized by the user.

The scope of information received depends on the user's settings and the third-party provider.

We automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- IP address;
- browser type;
- operating system;
- device identifiers;
- language settings;
- application version;
- crash reports;
- diagnostic information;
- network information;
- timestamps.

This information helps us maintain security, improve performance, diagnose technical issues, and support the Services.

## 5.11 Usage Information

We collect information about how the Services are used, including:

- features accessed;
- interactions with AI systems;
- marketplace activity;
- storefront visits;
- API usage;
- messaging activity;
- session duration;
- navigation patterns;
- performance metrics.

Payment transactions are generally processed through authorized third-party payment providers.

- payment status;
- transaction identifiers;
- billing history;
- subscription information;
- currency;
- invoice information.

## 5.13 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- improve security;
- measure performance;

- support optional personalization.

Unless specifically required for a feature you choose to use, we do not intentionally request or encourage users to provide:

- religious beliefs;
- trade union membership;

If such information is inadvertently provided, we will process it only where permitted or required by applicable law and encourage users to avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through the Services.
