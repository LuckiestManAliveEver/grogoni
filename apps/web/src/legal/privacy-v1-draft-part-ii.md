# Grogoni Privacy Policy

# 7. Legal Bases for Processing Personal Data

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, Grogoni processes Personal Data only where a lawful basis exists.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on one or more of the following legal bases.

---

## 7.1 Performance of a Contract

We process Personal Data where necessary to provide the Services requested by the user, including:

- creating and managing accounts;
- authenticating users;
- operating storefronts;
- enabling messaging;
- providing AI Business Agents;
- processing marketplace transactions;
- synchronizing data across devices;
- responding to customer support requests;
- maintaining subscriptions.

Without certain Personal Data, we may be unable to provide requested Services.

---

## 7.2 Compliance with Legal Obligations

We process Personal Data where necessary to comply with legal obligations, including obligations relating to:

- accounting;
- taxation;
- anti-fraud measures;
- consumer protection;
- lawful requests from competent authorities;
- security incident reporting;
- record retention.

---

## 7.3 Legitimate Interests

Where permitted by law, we process Personal Data where necessary for our legitimate interests, provided those interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of users.

Examples include:

- maintaining platform security;
- detecting abuse and fraud;
- preventing unauthorized access;
- improving system reliability;
- monitoring service performance;
- developing new platform features;
- conducting internal analytics;
- enforcing our Terms of Service;
- protecting users and the Services.

Where required, we conduct appropriate balancing assessments.

---

## 7.4 Consent

Certain processing activities rely upon the user's consent.

Examples include:

- optional contact synchronization;
- optional marketing communications;
- optional analytics cookies where required;
- optional social media integrations;
- optional personalization features.

Where processing is based on consent, users may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

---

## 7.5 Protection of Vital Interests

In exceptional circumstances, we may process Personal Data where necessary to protect the vital interests of an individual or another natural person.

---

# 8. How We Use Personal Data

We use Personal Data to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.

Examples include:

- providing requested functionality;
- authenticating users;
- operating AI Business Agents;
- enabling communication between users;
- maintaining storefronts;
- processing marketplace activity;
- supporting integrations;
- improving system reliability;
- detecting fraud;
- protecting platform security;
- complying with legal obligations.

We do not sell Personal Data.

---

# 9. AI Processing

Artificial intelligence is a core component of the Services.

Accordingly, Personal Data may be processed to:

- answer user requests;
- generate responses;
- summarize conversations;
- organize business information;
- retrieve catalogue information;
- automate workflows;
- classify uploaded information;
- assist with customer communications;
- improve AI performance where legally permitted.

The extent of processing depends upon:

- the selected AI model;
- user configuration;
- enabled features;
- applicable legal requirements.

---

## 9.1 Human Oversight

Although AI systems automate certain activities, users remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before relying upon it for important decisions.

Grogoni may involve appropriately authorized personnel in reviewing AI interactions where necessary to:

- investigate abuse;
- resolve support requests;
- improve platform reliability;
- comply with legal obligations;
- protect users and the Services.

Access is limited to personnel with a legitimate business need and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

---

## 9.2 AI Model Providers

Depending on the Services used, AI requests may be processed:

- using models operated by Grogoni;
- through open-source models deployed by users or Grogoni;
- by authorized third-party AI providers selected by the user or integrated into the platform.

Where third-party AI providers process Personal Data on our behalf, appropriate contractual safeguards will be implemented where required by applicable law.

---

# 10. Contact Synchronization

Users may choose to synchronize contact information from supported address books or connected services.

Contact synchronization is optional.

Where enabled, synchronized information may be used to:

- identify existing users;
- simplify communication;
- improve network discovery features;
- support business communications;
- facilitate customer engagement.

We process synchronized contact information only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise authorized by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis to share contact information where required by applicable law.

---

# 11. Social Media Integrations

If users connect supported third-party platforms, Grogoni may process information necessary to:

- authenticate users;
- send or receive authorized messages;
- synchronize business information;
- publish catalogue information;
- manage connected business accounts;
- enable customer communications.

Users may disconnect integrations at any time through available account settings, subject to any technical limitations of the third-party provider.

---

# 12. Receipt Processing and OCR

Where users upload receipts, invoices, or business documents, OCR technology may extract information to support features such as:

- bookkeeping;
- inventory management;
- product identification;
- expense tracking;
- transaction organization;
- accounting workflows.

Extracted information may be stored with the user's account to provide the requested functionality.

Users should review extracted information for accuracy before relying upon it.

---

- listings;
- purchases;
- installations;
- ratings;
- reviews;
- support requests;
- licensing;
- publisher information.

Marketplace information is processed to facilitate transactions, operate the Marketplace, maintain security, and enforce applicable policies.

# 14. Business Analytics

- generate aggregated statistics.

Personal Data may be processed to:

# 17. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to support the operation of the Services.

Categories may include:

### Essential Cookies

Required for authentication, security, and operation of the Services.

### Functional Cookies

Remember user preferences and settings.

### Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with the Services.

Where required by applicable law, analytics cookies are used only after obtaining appropriate consent.

### Performance Cookies

Assist in identifying reliability and performance issues.

### Personalization Cookies

Support optional customization features where enabled by the user.

Users may manage cookie preferences through available settings or their browser configuration, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

We may share Personal Data only where necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, or protect legitimate interests.

Recipients may include:

- cloud infrastructure providers;
- payment processors;
- authentication providers;
- messaging providers;
- hosting providers;
- analytics providers;
- customer support providers;
- professional advisers;
- auditors;
- competent public authorities where legally required.

---

## 18.1 Service Providers

Service providers processing Personal Data on our behalf are required, where applicable, to:

- process Personal Data only under documented instructions;
- implement appropriate security measures;
- protect confidentiality;
- comply with applicable data protection laws;
- delete or return Personal Data upon completion of services where appropriate.

---

## 18.2 Business Transfers

If Grogoni undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, Personal Data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Users will be informed where required by law.

---

## 18.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose Personal Data where necessary to:

- comply with applicable law;
- respond to lawful requests by competent authorities;
- protect legal rights;
- investigate fraud;
- enforce our Terms of Service;
- protect users or the public from harm.
